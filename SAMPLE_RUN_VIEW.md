The example's completed groups are 3 + 4 + 12 + 4 = **23**. `in_progress` is not counted; no future conditions are pre-counted as complete. The fixed 72-marker denominator holds for solo, meeting, blind, cross-artifact, and deep-comb runs. `satisfied_by_rule` (e.g., “not PRD-shaped”) counts only once actually evaluated and documented. The dashboard should **distinguish the glyph** (`↪` rather than `✓`) and show the reason.

Counts in this panel are **measurements**, never a second, shifting completion denominator. Full sensitive source bodies should stay in the source corpus, not in telemetry. An evidence pointer needs a stable path/segment/time range; private raw text must not be broadcast through the dashboard.
